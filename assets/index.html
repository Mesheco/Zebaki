About

About Zebaki

Artisanal and small-scale gold miners often use mercury to extract gold, and much of it is lost to the air and to tailings. Zebaki helps a cooperative record each batch, estimate where its mercury goes, and see how a cleaner method would reduce those losses.

  • Who it's for: miners, cooperatives and programme staff tracking mercury losses.
  • How it works: an AI step reads the report, you check the extracted details, rules estimate the losses, and the batch is added to the cooperative's totals.
  • Limitations: loss factors are illustrative placeholders, not values taken from a specific study or measurements. Published global figures are listed in the README. Zebaki estimates losses; it does not measure mercury in air, water or soil.
